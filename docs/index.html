A little about me

I’m a marketing and creative professional working across beauty, lifestyle, and digital media. A graduate of St. Lawrence University, where I studied business and creative writing, I bring together strategic thinking, editorial sensibility, and hands-on experience in social media, brand marketing, and content production.

My approach is grounded in understanding both the brand and its audience, developing thoughtful creative strategies, and translating them into content that feels considered, relevant, and cohesive.
